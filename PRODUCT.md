# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no build step (chosen by user). Mobile-first, deployable on GitHub Pages.

## Users

People buying a personal gift (partner, father, friends, couple) for a loved one, mostly from a phone. Italian-speaking. They want the piece to carry a name, date, place or phrase.

## Product Purpose

Demo storefront for a friend's small business selling engraved steel bracelets. The demo has 6 models and must convince the friend (the owner) that the site looks professional and sells. If it goes ahead, the catalogue may grow.

## Positioning

Engraving is the product: every bracelet is made to carry a message. The site lets the visitor try their own text on the bracelet before asking for it.

## Operating Context

Orders are handled by contact (WhatsApp/email) in this demo; no cart, checkout or payments. Products are engraved steel bracelets with different bands (chain, rigid cuff, magnetic leather-look strap, couple set, etc.).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Brand name is provisional: "Menny's Bracelets" (user: "braccialetti di menny", in English). Must be trivial to rename.
- Site copy in Italian. Prices in EUR.
- 6 products only; catalogue data kept in one JS/JSON structure so it can grow.
- Product photos and some descriptive facts were taken as reference from almendra.store at the user's request. They are placeholders for the demo; the friend's own photos and wording must replace them before any real launch.
- No payments, no backend, no analytics.

## Brand Commitments

Provisional name only. No logo yet.

## Evidence on Hand

Reference photos downloaded from almendra.store (`assets/products/`). No testimonials, no review counts, no real shipping/guarantee terms from the friend: do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- The engraving is the hero: show text on metal, not generic jewellery sparkle.
- Mobile first, light and fast.
- Honest: only claims the friend can truthfully make (to be confirmed).
- Easy to swap content: all products live in one data file.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, 44px touch targets, keyboard-operable engraving preview, respects prefers-reduced-motion.
