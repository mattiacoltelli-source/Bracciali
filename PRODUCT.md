# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no build step in production (pages are plain files). Mobile first, deployable on GitHub Pages or any static host. (User delegated stack and all design decisions: "mi fido di te".)

## Users

People in and around Bologna choosing where to eat tonight or this weekend, mostly from a phone: couples, friends, families, groups with birthdays or graduations. They want to see the food, check if the place is open, and book or order by phone/shop in one tap.

## Product Purpose

Complete website for Ristorante Dong ("Fusion restaurant", Chinese and Japanese cuisine à la carte, via Russo 4, San Lazzaro di Savena, Bologna). Rebuilds the existing ristorantedong.it with the same real content, much better presented. Success: a visitor can see the menu with photos, know opening hours, and call to book, order takeaway or open the online shop in under a minute.

## Positioning

Two culinary traditions at one table, with a menu à la carte of both. The restaurant's own idea is the balance of opposites (yin and yang), with a modern, minimal Eastern interior (red lacquer wall, black leather, brush calligraphy).

## Operating Context

- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 12:00-14:30 and 19:00-23:00. Closed Monday. Special hours on holidays (check by phone).
- Bookings and takeaway/delivery orders by phone 051 625 5139, Tuesday to Sunday 10:00-18:30.
- Takeaway online shop: shop.ristorantedong.it. Delivery: Deliveroo. Private room available for events.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content (dishes, descriptions, hours, address, phone) comes from the existing site; nothing invented. No prices are published on the existing menu pages, so none are shown.
- No booking backend: booking is by phone. The site shows whether the restaurant is open on a chosen day and slot, then offers the call button.
- Legal pages (privacy, cookie, notes) link to the existing site; company data: Mizuumi SRL, P.IVA 03504171202.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Dong", tagline "Fusion Restaurant". Existing logo assets (white DONG logotype "Fine Asian Flavors", gold brush ideogram) and the real photography (vermilion wall, black lacquer, dark moody food shots). Palette follows the real interior: vermilion red, black lacquer, brush gold.

## Evidence on Hand

About 140 dish photos and 8 interior photos downloaded from the existing site into `assets/`. No reviews, awards or press: do not invent any. Photos belong to the restaurant; confirm usage rights before launch.

## Product Principles

- Food first: big, real photography, dark moody ground.
- Both traditions equal: Japanese and Chinese get equal weight and a shared switch.
- One tap to act: call, order, directions.
- Honest: only facts from the restaurant.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Contrast AA, 44px touch targets, keyboard-operable menu navigation and search, prefers-reduced-motion respected, semantic landmarks, Italian lang.
